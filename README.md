## 🎯 Objective

Analyze business sales data to uncover **revenue trends**, **top-selling products**, **high-value categories**, and **regional performance** — then translate that into a client-ready set of insights and recommendations.

## 🗂️ Dataset

📦 **[Sample Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)** (Kaggle)
9,994 order line items · 2014–2017 · US retail orders across **Furniture**, **Office Supplies**, and **Technology**

## 🔬 Analysis Covered

1. ✅ Data cleaning — dates, duplicates, profit margin calculation
2. 📈 Monthly & yearly revenue trend
3. 🏆 Top-selling products, sub-categories & categories
4. 💸 Discount vs. profit relationship
5. 🌍 Regional & state-level performance
6. 👥 Customer segment performance
7. 💡 Key insights & recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

> **📅 Seasonality** — Sales consistently peak every November–December.

> **📦 Category mix** — Technology & Office Supplies drive most of the profit; Furniture is sales-heavy but margin-thin.

> **⚠️ Loss-making sub-categories** — Tables and Bookcases lose money almost entirely due to discounts above ~20–30%.

> **🗺️ Regional gap** — The Central region has solid sales but the weakest profit margin of all four regions.

> **🏠 Segment opportunity** — Home Office shows the strongest margin efficiency relative to its sales volume.
