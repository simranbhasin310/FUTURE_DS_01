# FUTURE_DS_01 — Business Sales Performance Analytics

**Internship:** Data Science & Analytics, Future Interns
**Task 1:** Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Objective
Analyze business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-selling products, high-value categories, and regional performance — and turn that into a client-ready set of insights and recommendations.

## Dataset
[Sample Superstore dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final) (Kaggle) — 9,994 order line items, 2014–2017, US retail orders across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology categories.

## Tools Used
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Jupyter Notebook

## Headline Numbers
- **Total Sales:** $2,297,201
- **Total Profit:** $286,397
- **Orders:** 5,009
- **Overall Margin:** ~12.5%

## Analysis Covered
1. Data cleaning (dates, duplicates, profit margin calc)
2. Monthly & yearly revenue trend
3. Top-selling products, sub-categories & categories
4. Discount vs. profit relationship
5. Regional & state-level performance
6. Customer segment performance
7. Key insights & recommendations

## Key Insights
- **Seasonality:** Sales consistently peak every November–December.
- **Category mix:** Technology & Office Supplies drive most of the profit; Furniture is sales-heavy but margin-thin.
- **Loss-making sub-categories:** Tables and Bookcases lose money almost entirely due to discounts above ~20–30%.
- **Regional gap:** Central region has solid sales but the weakest profit margin of the four regions.
- **Segment opportunity:** Home Office shows the strongest margin efficiency relative to its sales volume.

Full charts, code, and detailed write-up are in the notebook.
